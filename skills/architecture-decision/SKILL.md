# Architecture Decision

Record:

1. Context, goals, non-negotiable constraints, and observable requirements;
2. Facts versus load-bearing assumptions and their falsifiers;
3. Complexity that must exist and its owner;
4. At least two alternatives with distinguishing evidence;
5. Decision, rejected alternatives, and why the explanation is not freely adjustable;
6. Consequences, migration, validation, and rollback.

Prefer simple common usage, hidden volatile details, explicit ownership, and the smallest complete design justified now.
